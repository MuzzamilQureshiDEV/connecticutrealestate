Relocation

Moving to Connecticut — or out of it

Whether you are leaving a metro for more space or heading somewhere new, the practical questions come before the property: which town, which schools, what it costs to actually live there.

Speak to a relocation specialist

Where to look

Starting points by region

Commuter belt

Fairfield County

Closest to New York, strongest rail links, the state’s highest price points along the shoreline.

Space & quiet

Litchfield County

Land, lakes and antique housing stock, with longer drives to the interstate corridors.

Employment centre

Hartford County

Insurance and healthcare employment, central location, broad range of price points.

Coast & campus

New Haven County

Shoreline towns, university employment, a mix of dense city and suburb.

Every town in Connecticut assesses, taxes and zones differently. We will walk you through the specific numbers for your shortlist rather than quoting statewide averages that rarely match the street you are buying on.
